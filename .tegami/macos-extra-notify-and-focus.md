### Notify from the macOS extra when an agent needs you

The menu extra posts a Notification Center alert when an agent finishes, blocks, or joins Follow Up. Alerts fire when that state changes, not when you later open the extra.

### Bring the host terminal forward from the extra

Clicking an extra row or a notification focuses that pane and activates the terminal that is running Gardn.

### Keep control when focusing from the extra

Focusing an agent from the extra no longer flashes Take Control.
